Which fan bases travel?

Every franchise's home/road swing - and why it is the only version of this question that scores can answer.

What is being measured

For each franchise, take every home game and ask how many points it beat expectation by; do the same for every road game, from the team's own point of view. The difference between the two averages is the team's home/road swing. For an ordinary team the swing is twice the league home-field advantage: it gets the edge at home and gives it up on the road. The league-average swing over 1970-2025 is {{ facts.swing_league|f1 }} points.

A team whose fans travel well enough to neutralise road crowds should show a small swing - it gives up less on the road. But a small swing is also what a team with a quiet home crowd would show, and from scores alone the two can't be separated. The methodology page explains why. What the table below can say is whether any franchise's home/road split is unusual at all. The "z" column is the swing's distance from the league average in standard errors; with {{ facts.n_franchises }} franchises, one or two beyond ±2 is what chance produces.
