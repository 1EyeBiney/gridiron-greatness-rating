Has the balance shifted from turnovers to explosive plays?

Two analyses on the same one-row-per-game sample: which differential predicts the winner better (association), and how many points each is worth (exchange rate).

Association: win ~ explosive differential + turnover differential

Both differentials are standardized within season (z-scored) before fitting, so the coefficients are comparable across seasons with different raw scales. Three models are fit for every era and season: explosive-play differential (EPD) alone, turnover differential (TOD) alone, and both together. Log loss and accuracy are on the training sample.

Sensitivity: does the explosive-play definition matter?

The primary definition is a pass gaining 20+ yards or a rush gaining 10+. This repeats the association model for three alternative definitions (25/15, 20/20, and an EPA >= 2.0 threshold that removes some of the yardage tautology) and for the non-garbage-time variant of each, averaged across seasons.
