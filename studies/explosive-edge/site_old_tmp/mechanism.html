Strategy: explosiveness versus drive length

Within each era, the correlation across team-seasons between a team's explosive rate per play and its plays per drive. A negative correlation would mean more-explosive offenses run fewer plays per drive, consistent with Simms' argument that offenses now deliberately trade drive length for big-play upside.
