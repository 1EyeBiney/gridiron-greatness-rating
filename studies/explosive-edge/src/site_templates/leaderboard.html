Career explosive-pass-rate leaders

The measure is the quarterback's own completions of 20 or more yards divided by his own dropbacks: pass attempts, sacks taken, and scrambles, regular season 1999-2025, credited play by play to the passer on the field rather than to whoever started the game. Two companion columns say where the yards came from and what the same player did as a runner: the share of his explosive completions that travelled 20 or more yards in the air (the rest were caught short and run; air yards exist from 2006), and his explosive rate on his own rushes (scrambles and designed runs, 10 or more yards). Team-level explosiveness, all explosive plays over all scrimmage plays, is the offense table above; the three measures are kept apart on purpose.

Correction. The first version of this table, published in September 2026, labelled itself the same way but was computed differently: every explosive play his team produced in games he started, runs included, divided by the team's dropbacks in those games. An outside reviewer reproduced the published numbers and showed the label was wrong. That version also silently dropped {{ facts.qb_join_unmatched_v1 }} Rams, Chargers and Raiders team-games through a team-code mismatch in the join to the schedule file; the join now matches every regular-season team-game ({{ facts.qb_join_team_games|n }} of {{ facts.qb_join_team_games|n }}).
