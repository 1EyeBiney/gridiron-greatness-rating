Is explosive-play differential a skill, or is it noise like turnovers?

Turnover differential is known to be mostly noise from game to game. If explosive-play differential is more persistent - a team's rate in the first half of a season predicting its rate in the second half - it is closer to a repeatable skill, and a better thing to build a team around.
