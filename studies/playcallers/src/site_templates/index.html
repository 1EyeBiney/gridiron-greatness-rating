The Playcallers

Every NFL offense and defense from {{ facts.first_season }} to {{ facts.last_season }}, scored against the opponents it faced and credited to the man believed to have called its plays, then followed from job to job.

Who called the plays is this study's best guess, and it is labelled as a guess everywhere. No public record says who called plays for each team in each season. The starting point is the coordinator's title; head coaches believed to call their own plays are substituted, and marked unverified. The Who called plays page shows the guess for every team and season so that anyone who knows better can check it.

  • Ben Johnson and Mike Macdonald are as good as they looked.
