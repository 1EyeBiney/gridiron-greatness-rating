Gridiron Greatness Rating

How strong was every NFL team really, given what it did, who it did it against, and how strong its competitive environment was? This project rates every team-season since the 1970 AFL-NFL merger on a single, schedule-adjusted True Strength Rating, reported alongside (never blended with) a separate Accomplishment score for how successful the season actually was.

This is one of three studies. The Gridiron Greatness front page introduces all of them.

The two headline numbers

True Strength Rating (TSR) answers "how good was this team, really" - a network rating fit on every game a team played that season (regular season and postseason together), adjusted for the strength of every opponent faced, standardized so any two seasons from 1970 to today can be compared directly.

Accomplishment (ACC) answers "how successful was this season" - record, standing, division title, playoff wins, and the Super Bowl result. This is where the trophy is rewarded. TSR and ACC are always reported side by side and never combined into one number, because they answer different questions: the 2007 Patriots have the highest True Strength Rating on record and still didn't win the Super Bowl that year, while the 2011 Giants won it with a merely average regular season.

All figures on this site are also available as plain data tables and as CSV/JSON downloads - see Download the data. Nothing here is presented only as a chart.
